## 3. Judge Q&A Playbook: The 5 Toughest Questions & Winning Responses

### Question 1: "If entanglement doesn't transmit information, why do we use Bell pairs in quantum teleportation?"
* **Winning Answer:**  
  *"Teleportation transmits quantum information, but it does not transmit it faster than light. A Bell pair acts as a quantum resource that provides entanglement correlation. However, teleportation requires **two classical bits** transmitted through a conventional relativistic channel ($v \le c$) to instruct the receiver which Pauli correction ($I, X, Z, XZ$) to apply. Without those classical bits, the receiver's state is maximally mixed ($\rho = \frac{1}{2}I$), containing zero mutual information. Thus, teleportation obeys causality."*

### Question 2: "Could a non-linear modification to Schrödinger's equation allow superluminal signaling with ECHO?"
* **Winning Answer:**  
  *"Historically, physicists like Steven Weinberg explored non-linear quantum mechanics, and Polchinski proved that any non-linearity in quantum state evolution would indeed allow faster-than-light signaling and Everett phone calls. However, all experimental tests to date confirm quantum mechanics is strictly linear to extraordinary precision ($< 10^{-27}$). In Dr. Mahmud's machine, as we proved in Module 01 and Module 04, the gate operations follow standard unitary, linear transformations ($U^\dagger U = I$)."*

### Question 3: "Why did Dr. Mahmud leave her machine looping if she knew she was wrong?"
* **Winning Answer:**  
  *"Her note explicitly stated: 'Find the flaw before they bury it with me. — Z.' If she believed she had an infallible breakthrough, she would have published. Leaving the machine looping her decrypted admission word 'ECHO' was an invitation to forensic peer review. It was the act of a proud scientist who recognized a flaw she couldn't bring herself to publicly admit to rivals like Professor Vance."*

### Question 4: "What does the Bell-CHSH test prove that measuring in the Z-basis couldn't?"
* **Winning Answer:**  
  *"Measuring in only the Z-basis cannot rule out local hidden variables—it looks identical to pre-shared classical correlation (like two identical cards placed in envelopes). The Bell-CHSH inequality tests correlations across non-orthogonal, rotated bases. Classical theories cannot exceed a correlation value of $S \le 2$. Genuine quantum entanglement violates this bound up to the Tsirelson bound of $2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.828$. This proves the pair is genuinely quantum, but still non-signaling."*

### Question 5: "What if Sana Rao had not deleted the log?"
* **Winning Answer:**  
  *"The deleted log was the smoking gun of the classical channel. It would have recorded the timestamp of the classical packet transfer. By deleting it, the system appeared to operate with zero classical overhead, creating the optical illusion of instantaneous FTL teleportation."*

## 4. Live Judge Circuit Modification Defense Cheat Sheet

During Stage 2, the judges will alter one of your circuits and ask you to predict the outcome. Use these rapid transformation rules:

### Single Qubit Transformation Matrix
| Initial State | Applied Gates | Equivalent Operation | Final Measurement in $Z$-basis |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $|0\rangle$ | $H \to H$ | $I$ | 100% `0` |
| $|0\rangle$ | $X \to H \to H$ | $X$ | 100% `1` |
| $|0\rangle$ | $H \to X \to H$ | $Z$ | 100% `0` *(since $Z\|0\rangle = \|0\rangle$)* |
| $|1\rangle$ | $H \to X \to H$ | $Z$ | 100% `1` *(since $Z\|1\rangle = -\|1\rangle$, global phase)* |
| $|0\rangle$ | $H \to Z \to H$ | $X$ | 100% `1` *(since $HZH = X$)* |
| $|0\rangle$ | $X \to Z$ | $-iY$ (Bit flip + Phase) | 100% `1` |
| $|0\rangle$ | $Z \to X$ | $X$ *(since $Z\|0\rangle = \|0\rangle$)* | 100% `1` |
| $|0\rangle$ | $H \to Y \to H$ | $-Y$ | 100% `1` |

### Two-Qubit Entanglement Modification Rules
| Modification to Bell Circuit | New State | Measurement in $Z$-basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Original:** $H(0) \to CNOT(0,1)$ | $|\Phi^+\rangle = \frac{\|00\rangle + \|11\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}$ | 50% `00`, 50% `11` (Correlated) |
| **Add $X(1)$ after CNOT:** | $|\Psi^+\rangle = \frac{\|01\rangle + \|10\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}$ | 50% `01`, 50% `10` (Anti-correlated) |
| **Add $Z(0)$ before CNOT:** | $H \to Z = |-\rangle \implies |\Phi^-\rangle = \frac{\|00\rangle - \|11\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}$ | 50% `00`, 50% `11` (Same in $Z$, inverted in $X$) |
| **Invert CNOT (Target=0, Control=1):** | $|00\rangle \to H(0) \to CNOT(1,0)$ leaves q1 at 0 | 50% `00`, 50% `10` (Unentangled!) |
| **Measure in $X$-basis ($H \otimes H$):** | $(H \otimes H)|\Phi^+\rangle = |\Phi^+\rangle$ | 50% `00`, 50% `11` (Invariant) |
