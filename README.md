## Project Overview
This project investigates quantum measurement, entanglement, classical error correction, and quantum teleportation through the ECHO case study.

## Repository Contents
- `ECHO_Quantum_Investigation_Notebook.ipynb` — Quantum circuits, experiments, measurements, and module explanations.
- `ECHO_Case_Report.md` — Investigation theory, evidence board, results, alternative explanation, limitations, and references.
- `ECHO_Pitch_and_Defense_Guide.md` — Presentation outline and preparation for judges' questions.

## Modules
- **Module 01:** Quantum gates and measurement outcomes.
- **Module 03:** Bell-pair correlations and the CHSH test.
- **Module 05:** Classical repetition-code decoding of the message ECHO.
- **Stretch Module 06:** Quantum teleportation with and without correction operations.
- **Additional modules:** See the notebook for Modules 02 and 04 and their completion status.

## Main Findings
The reported experiments illustrate the difference between quantum correlation and communication. The teleportation experiment highlights the role of classical correction information. These results do not establish faster-than-light communication.
